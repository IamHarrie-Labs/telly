Today Connect
  1. 01

    Connect

    Sign in through Buffer. Buffer shows you exactly what Telly can do, and you approve it there.

    Telly never sees your Buffer password.

  2. 02

    Tell

    Add the title, date, venue, price and booking link. Or upload the flyer and Telly fills them in for you to check.

    Nothing moves on until the details look right.

  3. 03

    Schedule

    Read the announcement, reminder and day-of post, or press Listen to hear them. Then schedule all three.

    Times follow the event's own time zone, wherever you are.

  4. 04

    Change

    Plans changed? Edit the event. Telly lists the fixes and asks you first about any post you rewrote by hand.

    Nothing in Buffer changes until you say so.

  5. 05

    Confirm

    Approve the fixes. Telly saves them to Buffer, reads them back and tells you how it went.

    If anything didn't save, you'll see it straight away.
