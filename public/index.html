Today Connect
  1. 01

    Connect

    Sign in with Buffer. Buffer's own screen lists what Telly is asking for, and you approve it there.

    You sign in on Buffer. Telly never sees your password.

  2. 02

    Tell

    Type the title, date, venue, price and booking link, or upload the flyer and check what Telly read from it.

    Check the details before you continue.

  3. 03

    Schedule

    Preview the announcement, reminder and day-of post. Press Listen to hear them, then schedule.

    Posts go out on the event's local time, wherever you are.

  4. 04

    Change

    When plans change, edit the event. Telly lists the corrections and asks first about any post you edited by hand.

    Nothing in Buffer changes until you say so.

  5. 05

    Confirm

    Approve the corrections. Telly saves them, checks the posts in Buffer and tells you what happened.

    You'll see what saved and what still needs attention.
